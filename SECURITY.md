# Security Policy

We take the security of our projects seriously. Thank you for helping us keep them and their users safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, pull requests or discussions.**

Report them privately instead:

1. Go to the **Security** tab of the affected repository.
2. Click **Report a vulnerability**.
3. Fill in the form with as much detail as possible.

### What to include

- Type of issue (e.g. injection, authentication bypass, data exposure)
- Affected repository, component, URL or version
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- The potential impact, including how an attacker could exploit it

## What to expect

| Step | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement of your report | within 3 business days |
| Initial assessment | within 10 business days |
| Fix or mitigation for confirmed issues | depending on severity, as soon as reasonably possible |

We will keep you informed about our progress. If you want, we will credit you once the issue is resolved.

## Scope

This policy applies to all repositories in the **ops-lane** organisation and the services they run, unless a repository has its own `SECURITY.md`.

Out of scope:

- Denial-of-service attacks or high-volume automated scanning
- Social engineering or physical attacks
- Vulnerabilities in third-party services we do not control
- Reports without a demonstrable security impact (e.g. missing best-practice headers without an attack scenario)

## Safe harbour

We will not take legal action against anyone who reports a vulnerability in good faith and in line with this policy, provided they:

- make a reasonable effort to avoid privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption;
- only access the data needed to demonstrate the vulnerability;
- give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.
